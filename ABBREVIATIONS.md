# Abbreviations

Sources for `author_abbreviations.csv` and `work_abbreviations.csv`. Every abbreviation ends in a period (the reader
rejects others).

- **Lewis & Short** (authors and works): the `<author>` of citations in `data/dictionary/lexica/lat.ls.perseus-eng2.xml`
  and the work abbreviation after it, found by CTS URN in `<bibl n>`. Single-work authors have no work abbreviations. 
  E.g. Phaedrus: `Phaedr.`
- **Perseus Catalog** (authors only): "Abbreviations" on the author's page. E.g. Phaedrus: `Phaed.`, `Phaedr.`
- **TLL Index of Sources** (authors and works): the "notae" column of the
  [index](https://badwtll-live.srv.mwn.de/index/main.html), in small caps. Single-work authors have no title.
  E.g. Phaedrus: `Phaedr.`
- **Not used:** OCD and OLD (paywalled).
